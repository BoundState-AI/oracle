## 17. DOE vocabulary refresher

For Tuesday:

Effect

Predicted response change from low → high level.

Coefficient

Regression weight.

For two-level factors coded -1 to +1:

coefficient = effect / 2

Sign

Positive:

response increases moving low → high

Negative:

response decreases moving low → high

Always relative to response definition and coding.

Standardized effect

Effect relative to statistical uncertainty.

Essentially a t-type statistic.

P-value

Tests approximately:

null hypothesis: term = zero

Do not say:

"P = 0 means zero chance random."

Minitab may display 0 due to rounding.

Say:

"highly statistically significant"

Interaction

Not:

"two variables are bad together"

But:

the effect of one factor depends on the level of another

Joseph has tattooed on his arm a passage from A Farewell to Arms beginning:

"If people bring so much courage to this world…"

He first encountered the passage around age seven in a comic-book context involving Green Arrow's sidekick and heroin addiction, then later found Hemingway.

Joseph is a major Hemingway fan.

Interpretive importance:

A severely abused boy encountered a worldview in which:

the world breaks everyone
some become strong at broken places
some refuse to break

Possible childhood conclusion:

"Then I will become unbreakable."

That may have become a life project.

Low-Fi asks a different question:

"What if I stop volunteering to be broken?"
